Concept & pitch

Kashmir Kisan Saathi · Team hackathon concept

Concept stage

Kashmir Kisan Saathi is a team concept for a platform that connects farmers in Kashmir directly with buyers, starting with crops like apples, walnuts and saffron. The plan covered price tiers, grading, admin verification, transport agents, cold storage, weather alerts and voice support in Kashmiri, Urdu and Hindi, all with a farmer-first approach. We structured the pitch as problem, solution, features, impact and feasibility. It has not been built or deployed.
